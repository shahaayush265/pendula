About Pendula

Pendula is a real-time motion tracking and physics visualization tool.

It captures your raw 2D mouse or stylus movements and automatically determines your swing's primary angle using Principal Component Analysis (PCA).

Simultaneously, a live machine-learning optimization algorithm runs in the background to fit your imperfect human motion to the ideal mathematical equation for Damped Simple Harmonic Motion:

x(t) = A * e^(-γt) * cos(ωt + φ)

How to interpret the tracker:

  • Blue Trace: The raw input data from your mouse/stylus.
  • Orange Trace: The Live Predictive model (calculating rapidly during your swing).
  • Red Trace: The Final Post-Processed fit (calculated using rigorous simulated annealing once the pen stops).

Try placing your drawing tablet pen on its side and giving it a gentle nudge. You will see its natural decay perfectly match the math!

Known Cheats & Exploits

How to fool a math engine

  • The Straight Line: If you draw a straight line, the engine drops the Angular Frequency (ω) to 0, turning the SHM equation into a pure exponential decay.
  • The Spiral: Pendula calculates 1D shadows of your 2D motion. The 1D shadow of a shrinking circle is mathematically identical to a damped sine wave.
  • The Figure-8: Just like a spiral, repetitive 2D loops project a chaotic but highly periodic 1D shadow that can trick the curve fitter.
